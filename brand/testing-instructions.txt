The app finds AI-generated product images, places the official EU AI Act
label on them, and keeps an audit trail of every decision.

BEFORE YOU START
Use a development store with at least one product that has 2-3 images.
No credentials are needed. Access is free for 7 days from install, with
no card and no subscription, so the full app is available immediately.

SETUP
1. Install the app on your development store.
2. You will land on Terms of use. Tick "I have read and understood these
   terms" and press "Accept and continue". This gate is intentional -
   nothing else is reachable until it is accepted.
3. On Products, press "Scan catalog". The scan reads each image's
   provenance metadata and lists your products.

LABEL AN IMAGE
4. Press "Open" on any product.
5. Under "Which images are AI-generated?", tick one or more images. A
   label appears on the preview immediately; unticking removes it.
6. In the toolbox on the right, choose a Label type and Label style, drag
   the size slider, and drag the label around the image. It always stays
   at least 20 px from every edge.
7. Press "Apply to selected images" to save.

SEE IT ON THE STOREFRONT
8. In your store admin go to Online Store > Themes > Customize.
9. Open App embeds in the left sidebar and enable "AI disclosure".
10. Save, then view the product page you labelled. The label appears on
    the image in the position and size you chose.

AUDIT TRAIL
11. Open "Audit trail". Every decision is listed with a hash chain.
    "Export CSV" downloads the full record.

BILLING
12. Open "Plan". It shows days remaining in the free trial and a single
    $6.99/month plan. "Choose a plan" opens Shopify's own plan selection
    page at /charges/<app-handle>/pricing_plans, because this app uses
    Shopify App Pricing and must not create charges through the Billing
    API. Charges are live, not test.

    Note: on our dev store that page returns 404, matching the documented
    behaviour for a draft app still in review. The app performs the
    documented redirect with target="_top".

TWO THINGS THAT LOOK LIKE BUGS BUT ARE NOT
- Images usually come back as "Needs review" rather than being detected
  automatically. Most images carry no C2PA/IPTC provenance metadata, and
  the app never treats missing metadata as proof an image is not AI. It
  asks you instead. Silently marking an unknown image as "not AI" would
  under-disclose, which is the failure this app exists to prevent.
- On a large catalog the scan stops after about 20 seconds and reports
  partial progress. Press "Scan catalog" again to continue. Images already
  assessed are not re-downloaded, so each run gets further. This bound
  exists so the request cannot hang.
