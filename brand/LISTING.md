## Brand

| | |
|---|---|
| Blue | `#2B5CF6` |
| Type | Avenir Next — Demi Bold for the wordmark, Medium for supporting copy |
| Mark | Two flat shapes: a block for the product image, a pill beneath for the label |

The mark carries no letterform, so it survives being scaled to a 48px list row
where a wordmark would not. It avoids EU flag blue and any star motif on
purpose — the app already ships the Union's own icon pack, and borrowing its
visual identity on top of that would suggest an endorsement that does not
exist.

## Listing copy

**Name** — `EU AI Icons and Labels Act` (26 characters; Shopify's limit is 30)

**Tagline** — Label AI-generated product images with the official EU marks.

**Description opener**

> From 2 August 2026, Article 50(4) of the EU AI Act requires deployers to
> disclose AI-generated or AI-manipulated images that could pass for real
> photographs. This app reads the provenance metadata on your product images,
> asks you to confirm what a machine cannot determine, places the official EU
> label exactly where you want it, and keeps a tamper-evident record of every
> decision.
>
> Independent product. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the European Union.
> Compliance tooling and audit records — not legal advice, and no guarantee of
> compliance.

## Before submitting

- **Screenshots must show the actual UI.** Requirement 4.4.4/4.4.5, enforced
  since 26 March 2026: images that contain only a logo are rejected, and each
  image must show a different feature or state. Take them from the products
  list, the editing dashboard, the audit trail and the storefront result.
- **Do not imply endorsement.** The listing must not suggest the app is
  official, certified, or a compliance guarantee. The disclaimer above belongs
  near the top of the description, not buried.
- **Name risk.** "EU AI Icons and Labels Act" reads like the title of
  legislation, and Shopify asks that names lead with a distinctive brand
  identifier rather than describing function. Reviewers may push back, and it
  sits awkwardly beside a terms of service that disclaims official status.
  Alternatives that keep the searchable keywords: `AILabel — EU AI Act`,
  `Disclose — EU AI Act`, `Marque — EU AI Act`.

## Design notes

The badge in every asset uses the opaque `-solid` artwork. The pack's
"transparent" variants draw the pill at 50% alpha, which reads as washed-out
grey against light photography and turns to mush when scaled to 48px.

`product-carrots.png` is a flat illustration drawn by the asset script, not a
photograph. It is fine for the banner, where it demonstrates where the label
lands, but **swap in a real AI-generated product photo before submitting** if
you want the feature image to represent genuine output. Screenshots of the app
itself must be real UI regardless — requirement 4.4.4/4.4.5.
