Subject: 1.2.2 — prompted for a myshopify URL after approving, fixed

Thank you for retesting and for the recording. You are right, and the cause was
ours.

WHAT WAS WRONG

The subscription's returnUrl was our app's own URL,
https://eu-ai-labels.onrender.com/app/billing. That address carries no shop,
host or embedded parameter, so a merchant arriving on it after approving cannot
be identified. Our authentication finds no shop, redirects to the login path,
and the login form asks for a myshopify domain — which is the page in your
recording. A merchant who has just paid is dropped outside the admin and handed
a form they cannot usefully complete.

WHAT WE CHANGED

returnUrl is now the app's URL inside the Shopify admin:

  https://admin.shopify.com/store/<store>/apps/<app-handle>/app/billing

Approving now returns the merchant to the app's Plan page in the embedded
admin, with Shopify supplying the parameters that make the session resolvable.
A test asserts the app handle in that URL matches shopify.app.toml, so a wrong
handle cannot silently send a paying merchant to a 404.

VERIFIED

We have confirmed the destination loads correctly in the embedded admin: the
Plan page renders with the plan, price and remaining trial days, and no login
form appears at any point.

One note on our development store: it has no payment method on file, so the
Approve button is disabled there and we cannot complete the final approval
ourselves. We have verified everything either side of that step — the charge is
requested correctly, the approval screen shows the right plan and trial, and
the return destination resolves in the admin.

Deployed and ready for re-review.
