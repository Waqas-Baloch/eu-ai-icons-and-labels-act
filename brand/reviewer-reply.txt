Subject: 1.2.2 — 401 on subscribe, fixed and verified

Thank you for the report and the screencast. Both failures were ours and both
are fixed. Here is what was wrong and what we changed.

THE 401 ON SUBSCRIBE

The subscription itself was never rejected by Shopify. Our app called
billing.request(), which throws a redirect to the charge approval screen. For a
fetcher submission that redirect is returned as a 401 carrying
X-Shopify-API-Request-Failure-Reauthorize-Url — an App Bridge instruction
rather than a failure. We had recently added a global error boundary that
rendered any thrown response as an error page, so it turned that instruction
into a page reading "401 — Unauthorized" and the redirect never happened.

Fixed in two parts:

- The subscription is now created explicitly with appSubscriptionCreate, and
  the confirmation URL is returned to the page as data. The page moves the top
  frame to it and also renders it as a visible link, so the merchant reaches
  the approval screen whether or not the automatic navigation is permitted.
  Nothing depends on a status code being interpreted correctly.

- The error boundary no longer renders 401 or 410 as errors. Both are App
  Bridge protocol: 401 asks it to move the top frame, 410 to retry with a fresh
  session token.

THE SECOND FAILURE IN YOUR SCREENCAST

The recording also shows "accounts.shopify.com refused to connect". Our
/auth/login route called shopify.login() from its loader, which starts OAuth as
soon as a request carries a shop parameter. Inside the admin frame that is
unreachable, since the OAuth page cannot be framed. OAuth now starts only from
an explicit POST, and an embedded request is sent back into the app so token
exchange establishes the session.

VERIFIED

We have re-tested on our development store. Pressing Subscribe now reaches the
Shopify approval screen showing "Plan: Unlimited, $6.99 USD every 30 days" with
the remaining free-trial days applied, so a merchant who subscribes early is
not charged before their trial would have ended.

Both fixes are deployed. We are ready for re-review.
